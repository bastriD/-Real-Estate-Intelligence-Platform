# 1. Purpose

This document defines the business objectives of the Real Estate Intelligence Platform.

It establishes the expected business outcomes, measurable success criteria, stakeholder value, and strategic priorities that guide the design and evolution of the platform.

These objectives ensure that technical decisions remain aligned with business needs throughout the project lifecycle.

---

# 2. Business Problem

Searching for real estate remains a fragmented and time-consuming process.

Potential buyers and investors often need to:

- Browse multiple real estate websites.
- Compare hundreds of listings manually.
- Track price changes over time.
- Evaluate neighborhoods using external sources.
- Monitor newly published properties.
- Estimate investment potential.

These repetitive tasks increase the time required to identify suitable opportunities and reduce decision quality.

---

# 3. Business Vision

Provide a unified digital platform capable of transforming scattered real estate information into actionable intelligence.

The platform should simplify property discovery while supporting informed purchasing and investment decisions.

---

# 4. Strategic Business Objectives

## Objective 1 — Centralize Property Information

Aggregate listings from multiple sources into a single platform.

Expected benefits:

- Reduced search time
- Improved visibility
- Better comparison

---

## Objective 2 — Improve Decision Making

Provide analytical tools that help users evaluate properties objectively.

Examples include:

- Price evolution
- Market indicators
- Neighborhood statistics
- Investment metrics

---

## Objective 3 — Personalize User Experience

Adapt recommendations based on user preferences.

Capabilities include:

- Saved searches
- Intelligent recommendations
- Personalized alerts
- Preference learning

---

## Objective 4 — Automate Repetitive Tasks

Reduce manual effort through automation.

Examples:

- Listing monitoring
- Price tracking
- Daily reports
- Notifications
- Data collection

---

## Objective 5 — Deliver Actionable Insights

Move beyond listing aggregation by generating valuable business intelligence.

Examples:

- Best opportunities
- Market trends
- Investment scoring
- Comparative analysis
